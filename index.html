Mount Juliet Estate is located in Thomastown, Co. Kilkenny, Ireland, about 1.5 hours south of Dublin and 30 minutes from Kilkenny City. The Eircode is R95 NX10. Follow signs for Mount Juliet Estate off the N9/N10 Waterford road. Ample free parking is available on site.

View Mount Juliet Estate on Google Maps
By car: Take the M9 motorway south from Dublin towards Waterford and exit at junction 8 (Thomastown). The estate is 2 km from the exit.

By train: Irish Rail operates regular services from Dublin Heuston to Kilkenny (approx. 1.5 hrs). A taxi from Kilkenny City takes around 30 minutes.
